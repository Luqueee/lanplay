A7.1, the clock rate audit. The conditions every figure in this directory was
taken under, filed beside the numbers because a rate quoted without them is a
number somebody will later compare against a run taken on a different radio.

The preflight before the arm, replayable from preflight-window.csv at 1000 ms:

  PASS  +1.30 dB projected over 1500 s against a 3.0 dB budget, channel 100 at
        80 MHz, rate's halves within a factor of 1.00
  window  signal median -58 dBm, -60 to -57, sd 0.58, noise -94 dBm
          negotiated rate median 648 Mbps, 137 to 720
          movement +0.052 +-0.092 dB/min, scatter 0.58 dB about it
          120 rows of 120 due, 100.0 per cent

1500 seconds and not 1200, because the gate ran 90 + 90 + 1200 consecutively and
the projection has to span what is about to happen rather than the longest single
arm inside it.

A first attempt at this window was refused rather than reported, and the reason is
a harness defect worth recording. radio-preflight.sh's EXIT trap runs
`pkill -f target/release/radio-sample`, which cannot tell its own sampler from a
sibling's, so a concurrent preflight finishing 50 seconds into this one killed it
and the gate correctly refused with `radio-sample exited 143` instead of reporting
a partial window. audio-e2e-gate.sh already avoids exactly this on the host by
ending scheduled tasks by name rather than by image; the same reasoning has not
been applied to the sampler on this side. Reported and not fixed: A7.1 changes no
behaviour, and a preflight is not a thing to edit between two arms of one
measurement.

The gate's own readings, taken before the first arm and after the last and never
inside one:

  before  -58 dBm signal, -94 dBm noise, 612 Mbps, channel 100 at 80 MHz
  after   -63 dBm signal, -94 dBm noise, 576 Mbps, channel 100 at 80 MHz

Five decibels across the twenty-eight minutes the whole gate spans, against a
budget of three, and where inside those minutes it moved is not knowable from two
readings. That is a real limitation and it is stated rather than smoothed. What it
touches and what it does not is worth separating. It cannot move either rate: a
crystal's frequency does not depend on an association, the two rate figures carry
standard errors of 0.0001 and 0.0000 ppm, and the 90 s arm and the 1200 s arm
agree to 0.18 ppm on the source and 0.02 on the sink. It cannot move the samples
invariant, which closed to the sample on every arm. What it does touch is the
lateness, and the lateness is the noise floor of the growth measurement: a frame
concealed at its moment and counted late when its datagram turns up leaves the
concealed and late counters apart by however many frames are in flight, at 240
samples each. So a degrading link widens the error bar on the growth without
biasing it, and the +-75 samples quoted for the observed growth already contains
whatever those five decibels did.

loopback-control.receiver.out is the same receiver driven by audio-rtp-probe over
127.0.0.1 with no radio and no host at all. It is here because it is the only
population in which the samples invariant can be checked against a source with no
lateness in it: 0 late, 0 lost, 0 concealed, and `buffer growth 2752 samples held
2752 residual 0`. A run whose discards are all zero proves the identity's easy
half; the 1200 s arm, with 14985 late frames and a ceiling overrun of five, proves
the half that matters.
